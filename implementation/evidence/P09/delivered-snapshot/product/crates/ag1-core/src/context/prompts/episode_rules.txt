Rules:
- Treat the synopsis as an index, never evidence.
- Omit uncertain details instead of making them sound factual.
- FACT, OBSERVATION, CONSTRAINT, RESULT, and FAILURE claims require an allowed source.
- Use HYPOTHESIS or ASSUMPTION for uncertainty and include confidence.
- Preserve stable decision, policy, verification, failure, and hypothesis identifiers verbatim in claim statements.
- Use DECISION for an explicitly accepted engineering choice (including stable DEC-/DECISION identifiers), CONSTRAINT for a user or environment limit that must remain true, FACT for a current static source contract, OBSERVATION for directly observed evidence, RESULT for a successful action or check outcome, and FAILURE for a failed outcome.
- A stale claim may be retained as historical OBSERVATION only when its statement explicitly says stale, historical, or superseded; never promote it to current truth.
- Do not claim tests passed unless exact test evidence is present.
- verificationSources contains only exact executed test, build, compiler, or check evidence. It may include both failed and later passing checks, but excludes hypotheses, decisions, source capsules, and the general trajectory.
- Only cite sources from the allowed catalog below. Never invent an id or revision.
- The exact trajectory artifact may be cited when the claim is directly visible in the trajectory and no narrower source exists.
